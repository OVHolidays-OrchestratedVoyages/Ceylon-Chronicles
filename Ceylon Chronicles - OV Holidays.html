Welcome to Ceylon Chronicles

Sri Lanka’s cultural soul unfolds across misty highlands, ancient kingdoms, and sun-drenched coastlines. This 8-day journey traces the island’s living heritage — from the sacred streets of Kandy to the colonial bungalows of Nuwara Eliya, the clifftop serenity of Weligama, and the vibrant pulse of Colombo.

✦

Walk with local hosts through UNESCO World Heritage cities, sip freshly brewed Ceylon tea at its highland source, carve masks in a Galle Fort atelier, and cook alongside a legendary culinary host. Ceylon Chronicles is an intimate immersion into the stories, flavours, and craftsmanship that define this extraordinary island.

Kandy — The Last Royal Kingdom

Day 01: Arrival

“Ayubowan! Welcome to Sri Lanka” — Upon arrival at Bandaranaike International Airport, your OV Holidays representative will greet you and guide you through immigration to the exclusive Silk Route Lounge, where you can relax and refresh before your journey begins. Your dedicated chauffeur will then transfer you to Kandy.

Travel: Airport — Kandy  |  110 km  |  Approx. 3 hours

Optional: For a more exclusive arrival experience, opt for a Cinnamon Air domestic transfer — approximately 30 minutes by air and a 15-minute drive to the resort.

Check in at Taru Villas and spend the remainder of the day at leisure. Dinner is served at the hotel.

Day 02: Kandy City Walk

After breakfast, explore Kandy with a knowledgeable local host on a curated cultural walking tour. Begin at the iconic Dalada Maligawa (Temple of the Tooth) — the spiritual heart of the Kandyan Kingdom. Discover the unique architecture of the Paththirippuwa, Hevisi Mandapaya, and Magul Maduwa, and trace the fascinating fusion of indigenous Kandyan artistry with South Indian, Portuguese, Dutch, British, and Siamese influences. Walk through Raja Veediya, visit Biso Maligawa, Ehelepola Walawwa, and St. Paul’s Church, uncovering how Kandy absorbed and transformed foreign cultures while preserving its own. End at a local café, reflecting on the living heritage of this UNESCO World Heritage city.

Excursion: approx. 3 hours.

Return to the hotel and spend the afternoon at leisure.

Note: Comfortable walking shoes required. Dress modestly at religious sites — no shorts or sleeveless tops inside temples.

Nuwara Eliya — The City of Light

Day 03: Kandy — Nuwara Eliya · Tea Plantation

After breakfast, check out and journey to Nuwara Eliya, en route visiting a working tea plantation.

Travel: Kandy — Nuwara Eliya  |  83 km  |  Approx. 2 hrs 40 mins  |  Excursion: approx. 1 hour

Ceylon tea is one of the world’s most celebrated brews. Behind the scenes, meet skilled tea pickers, wander through lush highland plantations, and visit a processing factory to witness the craftsmanship behind each cup — then savour a freshly brewed pot at its very source.

Arrive and check in at Goatfell. Spend the afternoon at leisure.

Day 04: Explore Nuwara Eliya

Breakfast at the hotel. A free day to soak in the atmosphere of this remarkable highland town — once a British colonial retreat, still brimming with colonial bungalows, manicured hedgerows, and the world’s finest 18-hole golf course at altitude. Winding misty roads and lush green terraces offer views you will not soon forget.

Optional: Horton Plains National Park — a UNESCO World Heritage Site at over 7,000 feet. Trek across frost-kissed grasslands, past Baker’s Falls, to the edge of World’s End — a breathtaking 3,700-foot sheer drop. Watch for sambar deer, fishing cats, and if fortune favours you, the elusive Sri Lankan Leopard moving silently through the mist.

Note: Horton Plains entry is an optional supplement. Early morning departure recommended.

Weligama — Cliffs, Culture & the Indian Ocean

Day 05: Nuwara Eliya — Weligama

After breakfast, check out and journey south to Cape Weligama.

Travel: Nuwara Eliya — Weligama  |  257 km  |  Approx. 6 hours

Optional: Skip the drive and transfer by private helicopter — approximately 30 minutes by air with a 15-minute drive to the resort.

Set on a clifftop above the Indian Ocean, Cape Weligama is a Relais & Châteaux retreat across 12 acres of lush garden. Infinity pools, elegant villas, world-class dining, and the rhythm of the sea await.

Check in and spend the afternoon exploring the resort at your own pace.

Day 06: All Things Galle — Masks, Cooking & Culture

Breakfast at the hotel before departing for the Galle Dutch Fort for a day of cultural immersion.

Begin with a mask carving and painting session with Mr. Liyanarachchi — an expert archaeologist and passionate guardian of Sri Lanka’s coastal heritage. Meet the mask-making family, learn the centuries-old craft of carving vel kaduru wood, and create your own mask under the guidance of master artisans.

Then step into a charming heritage home within the fort for a hands-on cooking lesson with Mama — a legendary culinary teacher who has been sharing Sri Lanka’s kitchen secrets for over a decade. Visit the local market, source the freshest ingredients, and prepare a full Sri Lankan meal to savour in the peaceful ambiance of the fort.

Optional: Late afternoon, embark on a 3-hour sunset catamaran cruise from Mirissa along the southern coast — gourmet canapés, refreshing beverages, swimming, and stand-up paddleboarding included. (Available November to April only.)

Note: Sailing is seasonal and available from November to April only.

Colombo — The Vibrant Heart of Sri Lanka

After a leisurely breakfast, check out and drive north to Colombo.

Travel: Weligama — Colombo  |  154 km  |  Approx. 2 hrs 45 mins

Embark on a guided city tour of Sri Lanka’s commercial capital — a city where colonial grandeur meets modern energy. Explore the historic Fort and bustling Pettah market district, the leafy boulevards of Cinnamon Gardens, sweeping Galle Face Green, Viharamahadevi Park, and the recently opened Lotus Tower. Colombo’s harbour has drawn traders for over 2,000 years; today it pulses with corporate life, vibrant dining, and world-class entertainment.

Check in at Paradise Road Tintagel. Dinner is served at the hotel.

Optional: Indulge in a crab feast at Ministry of Crab — set in an elegant colonial-era space, this world-renowned restaurant celebrates Sri Lanka’s finest lagoon crabs. Choose from the signature Pepper Crab, Garlic Chili Crab, or fiery Sri Lankan Curry Crab.

Before You Go

Important Notes

Poya Days

Poya Days are full-moon observances and national holidays in Sri Lanka. During these days, alcohol sales are prohibited in most establishments, and some venues may have restricted hours. Plan accordingly.

Check-In & Check-Out

Standard check-in is 3:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available upon request and subject to availability — contact your hotel directly.

Belongings

Keep valuables in the hotel safe. Bags are provided for shoe storage at temples; always remove footwear before entering sacred spaces.

Travel Times

Road conditions in Sri Lanka can vary; travel times may differ based on traffic, road conditions, and weather. We recommend allowing extra time and remaining flexible with schedules.

Gratuities

Gratuities (tips) are not mandatory but are appreciated. A 10% gratuity on meals or 50–100 LKR per day for guides and drivers is customary and encouraged.

Dress Code

Pack light, breathable clothing for the tropical climate. For temple visits and cultural sites, dress modestly — cover shoulders and knees. Comfortable walking shoes are essential for excursions.
